Mumbles: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Mumbles: Stop Overthinking ("Mumbles", "we") is a wellness app that helps you step back from worrying thoughts. We built it so your thoughts stay yours.

The short version

  • Everything you write or say in Mumbles stays on your phone. We have no servers that store your thoughts, worries, check-ins, cycle data or anything else you enter.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking, no selling data. We don't use analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • Two things involve other companies, explained below: voice typing (Google) and purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat).

What stays on your device

Your roommate's look and name, the thoughts you catch, your worry jar, fact-checks, pocket lines, check-ins, journey progress, settings, and cycle dates (if you turn on Cycle sync). This is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

App lock: if you set a PIN, it is stored in your phone's secure storage. Fingerprint or face unlock is handled by Android; we never see your biometrics.

Voice recordings: if you record yourself saying a thought, the recording is saved on your phone only so you can play it back. It is never uploaded by us.

Voice typing (Google)

When you use voice typing, your phone's speech recognition service (usually Google) converts your speech to text. Depending on your phone, this may be processed on your device or sent to Google. That processing follows Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can always type instead.

Purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

Mumbles Plus is sold through Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have Plus. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase and subscription status from Google Play. It does not receive your thoughts or anything you write. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. Google Play's terms apply to payments.

Notifications

Reminders (worry time, fact-checks, the optional daily hello, trial reminders) are scheduled on your phone. No notification content is sent to us.

Backups

"Back up now" creates a file on your phone and opens Android's share sheet. Where you save it (Drive, email, Files) is your choice; that service's policy then applies. We never receive it.

Children

Mumbles is meant for adults (18+). It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Not medical care

Mumbles is a wellness tool, not therapy, diagnosis or treatment. If you are in crisis, use the helplines in the app or contact local emergency services.

Your choices

You can delete everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling. Cycle data can be deleted inside the app at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for meaningful changes, tell you in the app.
