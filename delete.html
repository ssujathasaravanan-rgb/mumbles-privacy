Delete your Mumbles data

App: Mumbles: Stop Overthinking (Android, package com.mumbles.app), by the Mumbles team. Contact: hello.mumbles@gmail.com.

Mumbles has no accounts and no servers that store what you write or say. Your thoughts, worry jar, fact-checks, check-ins, recordings, cycle dates, settings and app-lock PIN are stored only on your phone. You can delete them yourself at any time.

Delete everything on your phone

  1. Open your phone's Settings → Apps → Mumbles.
  2. Tap Storage (or Storage & cache) → Clear storage (or Clear data) → OK.

Or simply uninstall Mumbles: Android deletes all of its data with it. This deletes everything listed above immediately and permanently. We cannot recover it, because we never had a copy.

Backups: if you made a backup file with "Back up now", it is wherever you saved it (for example Google Drive or your Files app). Delete that file there.

Delete only your cycle data

In Mumbles, open Cycle sync (Room tab or Settings) and tap Turn off and delete cycle data.

Purchase records (Mumbles Plus)

If you bought Mumbles Plus, our purchase service RevenueCat keeps an anonymous app user ID and your purchase and subscription status, so your purchase can be restored. It contains nothing you wrote. To have it deleted, email hello.mumbles@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA., and is in your Google Play receipt email). We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is kept: Google Play keeps its own record of the payment, as required for tax and billing, under Google's privacy policy. Cancelling a subscription is done in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions; deleting data does not cancel it.

See also our privacy policy.
