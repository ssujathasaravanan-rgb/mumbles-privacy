Delete your data: Mumbles: Stop Overthinking

App: Mumbles: Stop Overthinking (com.mumbles.app) · Last updated: 11 November 2026

Mumbles has no accounts and no servers that store what you write or say. Almost everything lives only on your phone, so you can delete it yourself in under a minute.

Delete everything on your phone

  1. Open your phone's Settings → Apps → Mumbles.
  2. Tap Storage & cache → Clear storage (sometimes called "Clear data"), then confirm.
  3. Or simply uninstall Mumbles. Uninstalling deletes all of its data.

To delete only cycle data: in the app, open Cycle sync → Turn off and delete cycle data.

If you made a backup file with "Back up now", it's stored wherever you saved it (for example Drive or Files). Delete that file there.

What gets deleted

All of it, immediately and permanently: your roommate's look and name, caught thoughts, worry jar, fact-checks, pocket lines, check-ins, voice recordings, journey progress, settings, app-lock PIN and cycle dates. We never had a copy, so there is nothing left on our side.

Purchase records (if you bought Mumbles Plus)

We use RevenueCat to check whether you have Plus. It holds an anonymous app user ID and your purchase and subscription status, never your thoughts or anything you write.

  • To delete it: email hello.mumbles@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Mumbles data" and your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA., from your Google Play receipt email). We'll delete the RevenueCat record within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • What is kept: Google Play keeps its own order and payment records under Google's privacy policy. We can't delete those; Google keeps them for legal and tax reasons.
  • Deleting purchase records doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
